# Art credits

Everything in this repo can be shared: it's either CC0 or our own. Paths are relative to `game/`.

## Kenney (CC0)
`assets/kenney/` is cut from Kenney's "Tiny" packs ([kenney.nl](https://www.kenney.nl), CC0: free for any use, credit
appreciated but not required). The packs' tile sheets and licenses are in `art-source/kenney/`; rebuild with
`python tools/kenney_art.py`, then `python tools/make_room_tileset.py`.

| Pack | Used for |
|---|---|
| *Tiny Dungeon* | room walls and floors (recoloured per room), doors, the pit, treasure chests, the stairs down |
| *Tiny Town* | bushes, signs, bombs, the key (recoloured gold) |
| *Tiny Battle* | hearts, the pond |
| *Tiny Farm* | crates, rock piles |
| *Tiny Ski* | boulders |

The lever, the torch and the sparks on lit bombs are drawn by Claude in `tools/kenney_art.py`, in Kenney's colours.

## Palette
Most of our own sprites use colours from *Rosy 42* by **@PineTreePizza** (`art-source/palette.png`).

## Our own art
- `assets/sprites/chameleon/`: drawn by Claude as ASCII grids (`art-source/chameleon/*.txt`), based on
  the reference pictures in `art-source/chameleon/`, which were generated with **Grok**.
  Re-render with `python tools/ascii_sprite.py <txt> <png>`.
- `assets/sprites/red_fox/` and `assets/sprites/arctic_fox/`: drawn by Claude (`art-source/red_fox/*.txt` and
  `art-source/arctic_fox/*.txt`, generated by `tools/fox_frames.py`; the red fox is a recolour of the arctic fox).
- `assets/sprites/icons/`: menu icons drawn by Claude (`art-source/icons/*.txt`).
- `assets/sprites/beetle/`: the Giant Beetle boss, drawn by Claude (`art-source/beetle/giant_beetle.txt`, generated by `tools/beetle_frames.py`),
  and the beetle hero (`art-source/beetle/beetle_*.txt`, generated by `tools/beetle_hero_frames.py`).
- `assets/sprites/deer/`: the deer enemy's walk cycle, drawn by Claude (`art-source/deer/deer_walk.txt`, generated by `tools/deer_frames.py`).
- `assets/sprites/moose/` and `assets/sprites/peacock/`: the moose and peacock heroes, drawn by Claude
  (`art-source/moose/`, `art-source/peacock/`, generated by `tools/moose_frames.py` and `tools/peacock_frames.py`).
